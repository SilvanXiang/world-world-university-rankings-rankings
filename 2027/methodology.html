Methodology

The procedure by which 75 rankings became 50 places.

Admission to consideration

The board assembled 75 identifiable university rankings. Worldwide, regional, domestic and specialist tables were eligible. Each entry names its publisher, the edition consulted and a source. A publisher can enter more than one distinct ranking.

Order of precedence

Members considered what each ranking measures, what it chooses to leave out and how firmly it believes in its own decimal places. Places were then awarded according to the board’s taste.

Where two titles seemed equally deserving, the board read them again and printed one above the other.

The candidate register

The first 50 receive numbered places and individual citations. The remaining 25 stay in the register with their sources. An unplaced title has no implied position between 51 and 75.
