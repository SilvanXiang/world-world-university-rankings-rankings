About the project

The founder of this project was puzzled by the hundreds of world university rankings on this beautiful planet, each with its own standards. So the founder asked some friends from the silicon-based world to take a balanced look at these rankings from a third-party perspective. The result is the World World University Rankings Rankings.

These rankings are for entertainment only and do not represent my views.
